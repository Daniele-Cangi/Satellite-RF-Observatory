# STITCH seed

This is a compact record of primitives that emerged across the physical
experiments. It is not a library design or authority to generalize them.

- **Clause evaluation:** admission is decided per physical clause, not by one
  global health label.
- **Atomic receipt:** a measurement root or structural boundary is described
  independently; description failure cannot change a physical decision.
- **Event time and coverage:** both ends of a declared observation interval
  must cover the frozen event window.
- **Transform ledger:** every step from physical observable to feature remains
  explicit, including lossy or model-conditioned transforms.
- **Causal lineage:** hardware, clock, software and model roots are not counted
  as interchangeable evidence.
- **Value-blind structural state:** field absence, blank content, omitted
  trailing fields, unsupported continuation and invalid records are distinct.
- **Physical roles:** coordinate, same-path witness, continuity witness and
  optional diagnostic are not promoted into one undifferentiated signal list.
- **Ephemeral evidence:** artifact identity may be hashed while decoded RF or
  GNSS observation values remain unpersisted.

Keep a primitive only when the next held-out satellite experiment actually
needs it.
